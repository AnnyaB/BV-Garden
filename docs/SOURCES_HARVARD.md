# Research Garden — Harvard-style source catalogue
**Reference cut-off:** 12 November 2026. **Scope:** selective foundational and frontier reading for the 20-station game, not an exhaustive index of every AI paper ever written.

## How to interpret this document
- References use the Harvard author–date convention: author(s), year, title, publication or publication status, and publisher/DOI or primary-source link. Web resources have an access date of 12 November 2026.
- `[Peer-reviewed]` denotes a journal or established refereed conference; `[Preprint/position]` denotes material not claimed here as peer reviewed; `[Book]`, `[Primary archive]` and `[Official source]` are distinct types.
- The questions and miniature interactive experiments are **original educational exercises**, not reproductions of paper findings. Association with a chapter is curricular, not evidence that a named paper solves all its open problems.
- The last stage is an **unachieved theoretical frontier**: open-ended/general/superintelligent abilities are not established by the game, and future results cannot be cited as publications.
- Papers are organised by topic, with their associated chapter numbers. For historical chronology, refer to their publication years. Papers after the reference cut-off must be added only when published and checked.

## Research questions for future work (not citations or invented completed papers)
A. **Continual world-model revision:** Can an embodied agent detect a causal dynamics failure, revise only what failed, and preserve unrelated skills under long-horizon change?
B. **Unseen object/skill composition:** Can learned factorized interaction primitives generalize to genuinely unseen object combinations without task-specific annotations?
C. **Reliable planning under shift:** Can calibrated uncertainty, recovery, and abstention outperform strong model-based planning baselines without assuming access to accurate dynamics?
D. **Compute-efficient open-ended learning:** Can embodied agents autonomously design discriminative experiments and expand their representations within strict compute/safety constraints?
E. **Evaluation beyond benchmark memorization:** Can pre-registered OOD test suites, multiple seeds, and public negative results discriminate adaptation from leakage or shortcut learning?

These are proposals and remain open until a specific method, evaluation, and independently reproducible evidence exist. They are **not** evidence of AGI, ASI, consciousness, or hypothetical beyond-human intelligence.

## Quality and maintenance
- The list includes influential historical works and **selected** current sources, not every paper ever published. Coverage is deliberately bounded and tied to the game's curriculum.
- For any new 2026+ citation, confirm authors, venue, final title, version, paper status, and DOI against the publisher or accepted proceedings before adding it.
- The in-game toy simulations may be hand-coded and simplified. Do not cite performance or safety claims from toy mechanics as empirical research results.
