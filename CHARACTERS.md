## Curriculum and access boundaries
- The route is **01 Anya → 02 Bond → … → 19 Yor → 20 Loid**.
- Roaming to any station is possible, but it does not grant access to a locked lesson or bypass Stella/Tonitrus rules.
- The final Loid chapter concerns hypothetical open-ended/general/superintelligent intelligence; it is **not** a real-world demonstration of achieved AGI or ASI.
- Mr. Chimera is a canonical in-universe stuffed toy, **not a human character**. The roster is a selected set, not the complete franchise cast.
